# Workspace panes

On desktop, drag the divider beside Sessions or Files to resize the sidebar. Focus a divider and press Left or Right Arrow to adjust it in small steps. Press Home or double-click the divider to restore its default width. The Sessions header can collapse to a narrow rail and expand again. Widths and collapse state are remembered on this device. On narrow screens the workspace continues to use swipeable Sessions, Chat, Changes, and Files pages; desktop widths do not change those pages.
